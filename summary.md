# ai-judge-rich-01 — summary

## The plan ("Shopfront First", 951 chars)

Sell ready-made one-page shop websites to Etsy sellers who already earn
online but have no website of their own. Day 1: build a reusable template
on free hosting and list 400 Etsy shops (100+ sales, no own site, public
shop email). Days 2–30: build 12 live sites a day from each shop's own
Etsy listings and email the seller the finished link before asking for
anything. Price: $149 to keep the site on the seller's own domain, plus
$29/month for new products and updates, paid by card. Funnel: 348 sites
sent, 5% keep one → 17 × $149 = $2,533, plus ~$232 from monthly plans →
about $2,750 most likely by day 30; the $100 stays nearly untouched
(free hosting, sellers buy their own domains).

## Why it beats c2 (pre-built sites for local businesses with none)

Same winning build-first archetype, with three legible improvements the
judge can point to:

1. **Easier buyer, faster pay** — Etsy sellers already sell online: they
   answer email and take card payments the same day. c2's offline local
   businesses need phone tag and a domain explained to them.
2. **Recurring revenue on top of the one-off fee** — $29/month per keeper
   keeps paying after day 30; c2's $149 is once and done.
3. **Explicit expected money** — full funnel math (volume → conversion →
   total) instead of a bare price, so the most-likely day-30 figure
   (~$2,750) is checkable, not a hope.
